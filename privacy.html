Privacy policy

For the Topfkumpel app

In short
Topfkumpel needs no account, shows no ads and uses no analytics or tracking services. Your plants, watering data, points and settings are stored only on your device. Only when you have a photo analysed by the AI (Pro) is that photo sent off for analysis, and only after you have expressly agreed. For Pro, our server uses a subscription ID from Apple to check whether your subscription is valid. We never receive your name or your payment details.

Scope
This policy applies to the Topfkumpel app, its photo server and the website topfkumpel.github.io. It follows the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and, for users in the EU and the United Kingdom, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR and UK GDPR).
What data the app stores
The plants you add (name, species, location, room, pot size), when you watered and fed them, the results of light measurements and photo checks including small preview images, whether you have agreed to the terms of use and to photo analysis, your points, badges and settings. The app only stores what it needs for its features. This data stays on your device. We have no access to it. If you have iCloud Backup switched on, the data is included in it. Apple is responsible for that.
What the app doesn’t do
No ads, no analytics tools, no tracking across other apps, no selling of data. The app doesn’t ask for your name or email address, not even for the subscription, and doesn’t use your iPhone’s location services. It only uses the camera and your photos when you take or choose a photo yourself. The font is also built into the app and is not loaded from the internet.
Camera and photos
Before the app uses the camera or your photos for the first time, your iPhone asks for your permission. You can change it at any time in the iPhone Settings under Topfkumpel. The app scales the photo down, doesn’t read any location data from it and doesn’t send any.
Identifying plants and health check (Pro)
Only if you use these features and have agreed beforehand: the app sends the scaled-down photo and the necessary details about the plant (for example species, location, pot size, watering plan, recent watering days and measured light) via our photo server at Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc., USA) to the Topfkumpel AI. The Topfkumpel AI uses the AI model Claude from Anthropic (Anthropic PBC, USA). The model analyses the photo and sends back the result. Our server only forwards the request and stores neither the photo nor the result. For technical reasons the IP address of your device is transmitted too. To limit excessive requests, the server only stores an irreversibly encrypted value (hash) of it and deletes it after 2 days at most. So that only people with a subscription use the Topfkumpel AI, the app also sends the subscription ID from Apple (see “Subscription and payment”). According to its commercial terms, Anthropic doesn’t use the data to train its models and usually deletes it after 30 days at the latest. Content that violates Anthropic’s rules may be stored for longer. If you search for care information for a plant that is not in the library, only the plant name you typed goes this way. Cloudflare and Anthropic work on our behalf. They may only use the data for this analysis and must protect it at least as well as described in this policy.
Light meter (Pro)
For the light measurement you take a photo. The app only reads the exposure values (exposure time, aperture, ISO) and the brightness from it. The photo is not stored and doesn’t leave your device. Only the measured value is saved with the plant.
Reminders
Your iPhone schedules the watering reminders and sowing reminders itself. No data is sent to us or to third parties for this. You can switch them off at any time in the app or in the iPhone Settings. The same goes for the widgets on the Home Screen: they show information from the app and run only on your iPhone.
Holiday plan, backup and sharing
You send the holiday plan, the backup code and the “Share with friends” recommendation yourself, for example by message. Only then does this data leave your device, and only to the place you choose. The backup contains no photos.
Subscription and payment
You take out Topfkumpel Pro as a subscription through your Apple Account. Apple handles payment, invoicing, renewal and cancellation. We receive neither your name nor your email address or payment details, only a subscription ID from Apple (transaction number), the type of subscription and how long it is valid. The app stores this information on your device. When you use the Topfkumpel AI, the app sends the subscription ID to our photo server. The server uses it to ask Apple whether the subscription is valid and counts how many analyses have already been used this month or during the free days. For this it stores the subscription ID, the subscription status and this counter for 40 days at most, without photos and without any link to your name. Apple’s privacy policy applies to the purchase itself.
App Store
You download the app from Apple’s App Store (Apple Inc., USA). Apple processes data for this under its own privacy policy. We only receive crash reports if you have allowed sharing them with app developers in the iPhone Settings. They only contain technical details about the error.
Contact by email
If you write to us, we only use your email address and your message to reply to you. The emails are kept in a mailbox at Microsoft Outlook. We delete them as soon as your request has been dealt with and we no longer need them.
This website
The website topfkumpel.github.io is hosted by GitHub (GitHub Inc., USA, a Microsoft company). When you visit it, GitHub stores technical data such as your IP address for security reasons. The website itself uses no cookies and no analytics tools, and doesn’t load fonts from other providers. You can find out more in GitHub’s privacy statement (docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies).
Storage period
The data in the app stays on your device until you delete it or remove the app. Our photo server stores no photos. It deletes the subscription ID, the subscription status and the monthly counter after 40 days at the latest, and the encrypted value of the IP address after 2 days at most. Anthropic usually deletes photos and results after 30 days at the latest. Photos for the light meter are discarded immediately. We delete emails to us as soon as they have been dealt with.
Legal basis
We only have photos analysed with your express consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). Checking the subscription and the monthly counter serve to provide the subscription (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). The encrypted value of the IP address against excessive requests and the website logs at GitHub are based on the legitimate interest in secure operation (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). We answer emails because you ask us to (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR). All other data is processed only by you on your own device.
Transfers abroad
GitHub, Microsoft (Outlook) and Cloudflare are based in the USA and are certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and the Swiss-US Data Privacy Framework, which the EU and Switzerland recognise as providing adequate data protection. For Anthropic (USA), the transfer is safeguarded by the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses, which Anthropic provides for in its contract terms. Apple is itself responsible for the App Store and governs transfers in its own privacy policy.
Voluntary and without automated decisions
You don’t have to give us any data. The app also works without photos and without emailing us. The AI only gives care tips. There are no automated decisions with legal effect and no profiling.
Deletion and withdrawal
In the settings under “Data” you can delete all plants, photos, points and settings. If you remove the app from your iPhone, all data is deleted as well, except in an older iCloud backup. You can withdraw your consent to photo analysis at any time under Camera › Topfkumpel Pro. After that, no more photos are sent.
Your rights
You have the right to access, rectification, erasure and portability of your data and to withdraw consent. You can also object to processing or ask for it to be restricted. Because we don’t store any data about you on a server, there is usually nothing for us to hand over or delete. Still, feel free to write to us at any time at topfkumpel.app@outlook.com. You can also lodge a complaint with the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC) or, if you live in the EU or the UK, with the data protection authority of your country.
Children
Topfkumpel is suitable for all ages. The app requires no accounts and no personal details. Photo analysis by the AI is based on consent. Anyone under 16 needs their parents’ agreement for it. Please only photograph plants for analysis, not people.
Changes
If the app changes, for example through new features, we update this policy. For significant changes we ask for your agreement again in the app. You will always find the current version in the app and on this website. This is a translation of the German original.
