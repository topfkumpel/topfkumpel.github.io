Care tips are recommendations
Watering rhythm, amount of water, feeding, garden calendar, the guides to overwintering and pruning and the tips are general guidelines for the climate of Switzerland, Germany and Austria. If you live elsewhere, seasons and timings may differ. They have been compiled with care, but they are not a guarantee and not professional advice. Every plant, every location and every soil is different. Before watering, use the finger test to check whether the soil is dry, and adjust the plan if your plant responds differently. How well a plant gets through the winter depends heavily on variety, location and weather. When pruning, you are responsible for your own safety: wear gloves with toxic plants such as oleander or angel’s trumpet and only work on tall trees with a secure footing.
Edible plants and pest control products
The guides for vegetables, herbs and fruit are no substitute for professional advice. Only eat plants you can identify with certainty. If you use a pest control product, follow the instructions on the pack, including the waiting period before harvest.
Toxicity
The notes on toxicity to pets are based on public lists, above all the ASPCA list (aspca.org). They are not complete, not the same for every species and no substitute for professional advice. If a plant says “No verified information”, that doesn’t mean it is non-toxic. Even plants considered non-toxic can cause problems in larger amounts. Whether a plant belongs near children or pets is your decision. If you suspect poisoning, contact a doctor or a vet immediately. In Switzerland, Tox Info Suisse helps around the clock on 145, in Austria the poison information centre on 01 406 43 43, and in Germany the poison helpline of your federal state, for example 030 19240 in Berlin or 089 19240 in Munich. In other countries, call your local poison helpline or the emergency services.
