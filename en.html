Topfkumpel

Your plant buddy for balcony, garden and home

Free for iPhoneDownload on the App Store

Topfkumpel reminds you to water and feed your plants, adapts the plan to pot, location and season, and explains step by step how to grow vegetables, herbs and fruit. Seasonal advice follows the climate of Switzerland, Germany and Austria. Available in English, German, French and Italian.

What the app can do

  • A watering plan for every plantOver 250 plants, adapted to pot size, room and season.
  • RemindersA notification when watering or feeding is due.
  • Identify plants by photoTake a photo, have the species identified and the watering plan set up automatically. (Pro)
  • Health check and light meterThe AI checks leaves and soil, and the light meter shows whether your plant gets enough light. (Pro)
  • AI plant search and widgetsCare information for any plant that is not in the library, and on the Home Screen you can see what needs watering today. (Pro)
  • Help with problemsYellow leaves, pests or mould? The app shows you what is behind it and what helps.
  • Sprössli grows with youPoints, badges, pots and backgrounds that you unlock with good care.
  • Holiday planThe app writes the message for the person who waters your plants while you are away.

You can try the “Pro” features for free: 3 scans with the Topfkumpel AI and 2 light measurements, without a subscription.

Frequently asked questions

How do I change the language?

The app follows the language of your iPhone: German, French or Italian if your iPhone is set to one of these languages, otherwise English. You can change it at any time in the app under Settings › Appearance › Language.

The watering reminders are not arriving.

Check in the iPhone Settings under Notifications › Topfkumpel whether notifications are allowed. In the app you can choose the time of the reminder under Settings. In Focus or Sleep mode, notifications arrive later.

How do I take my plants to a new iPhone?

On the old iPhone, open Settings › Back up data and create a backup code. Send it to yourself, for example by message or email, and paste it on the new iPhone under Back up data › Load a backup.

Does Topfkumpel cost anything?

The basic features are free and without ads: watering plans, reminders, the library, awards, holiday plan and backup. With the “Topfkumpel Pro” subscription you also get the camera features (identifying plants, health check, light meter), the AI plant search and the widgets, as a monthly or yearly subscription. With the yearly subscription the first 7 days are free. Without a subscription you can try them out: 3 scans with the Topfkumpel AI and 2 light measurements are free. You can see the prices in the app.

How do I cancel Topfkumpel Pro?

On your iPhone, tap your name in Settings, then “Subscriptions” and “Topfkumpel Pro”. If you cancel at least 24 hours before it ends, the subscription does not renew. Pro stays active until the end of the paid period. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Does the app need an account?

No. Your plants are stored only on your iPhone. There is no account. For Pro, our server uses a subscription ID from Apple to check whether your subscription is valid, and for the free scans it counts with a random device ID. We never receive your name or your payment details.

What happens to my photos?

For plant identification and the health check, a scaled-down photo is analysed by the Topfkumpel AI once you have agreed. It uses the AI model Claude from Anthropic. Our server only forwards it and does not store it. The light meter only calculates on your iPhone, the photo does not leave your device. You can find out more in the privacy policy.

How accurate is the light meter?

It estimates brightness from the camera’s exposure values. That is easily enough to tell whether a spot is too dark, suitable or very sunny. It does not replace a calibrated measuring device. Best to measure during the day and point the camera from the plant’s spot towards the window.

How do I delete all my data?

In the app under Settings › Data you can delete everything. If you remove the app from your iPhone, all data is deleted as well. Only the random ID for the free scans stays in the iPhone’s keychain.
